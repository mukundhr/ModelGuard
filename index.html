Shows the largest accuracy drop under drift (97.6% → 75.7% under noise). Confidence also shifts, making failure detectable. This is the most honest model.

Random Forest

Maintains near-perfect accuracy (99.95% → 99.91%) even under heavy drift. Confidence barely moves — partial silent failure risk with slight entropy increase.

XGBoost

Highest raw accuracy (99.96%) with near-zero entropy. Confidence remains rigid at ~99.99% regardless of drift. Highest silent failure risk — the model never signals distrust.

Reliability Ranking

A composite score measuring how strongly each model reacts to drift. Higher sensitivity = the model signals when it's failing. Lower sensitivity = silent failure risk.

Ranking Methodology

The reliability score is the average absolute change in accuracy, confidence, and entropy between no-drift and maximum-drift conditions across all drift types.

Most Self-Aware: Logistic Regression

LR exhibits the highest sensitivity to drift across all metrics. When performance degrades, its confidence drops and entropy rises — making failure visible and actionable.

Partially Aware: Random Forest

RF shows moderate sensitivity. Under noise drift, entropy increases measurably, but the overall confidence remains high. There's a window where RF fails without adequate warning.

Silent Failure: XGBoost

XGBoost shows the lowest sensitivity score. Under every drift type, its confidence barely changes and entropy stays near zero. This is the textbook definition of silent model failure.

Methodology

Experimental design and technical details behind ModelGuard.

📊

Dataset

Credit Card Fraud Detection dataset with 284,807 transactions (492 frauds, ~0.17% positive rate). Split 60/20/20 into train, validation, and test sets with stratified sampling.

🤖

Models

Logistic Regression — linear baseline.
Random Forest — ensemble of decision trees.
XGBoost — gradient-boosted trees (300 estimators, max depth 6).

🔄

Drift Simulation

Noise: Gaussian noise N(0, σ) added to all features.
Scale: First 5 features multiplied by (1+intensity).
Bias: Constant shift added to all features.
Intensities: 0.0, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0.

📐

Calibration

Platt Scaling (sigmoid) — fits a logistic function to model outputs.
Isotonic Regression — non-parametric monotonic calibration.
Both fitted on the held-out validation set using CalibratedClassifierCV.

Metrics Explained

Three core metrics evaluate model reliability under shift.

MetricFormulaInterpretation
Accuracy Correct predictions / Total predictions How often the model is right — but does not reveal if the model knows when it's wrong.
Mean Confidence avg(max(P(y|x))) Average of the model's highest class probability. A confident-but-wrong model fails silently.
Mean Entropy −Σ p·log(p) Measures prediction uncertainty. Higher entropy = more uncertainty = the model signals it is unsure.
